Figure: Overview of the TARA framework. TARA-k (batch): CHSH measurement datasets are turned into feature vectors and conformal p-values against an LHV null manifold; a one-class detector and a KS test on p-value uniformity decide Quantum vs. Classical. TARA-m (streaming): each new measurement yields a conformal p-value that updates a betting martingale, and quantum behaviour is declared when wealth crosses 1/alpha (anytime-valid via Ville's inequality).
